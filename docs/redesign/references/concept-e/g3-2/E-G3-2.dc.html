Why A

  • Where the split came from: Concept C drew bold numerals on phones and lighter ones on wide screens; E copied both. Nobody chose it for E.
  • One identity (DESIGN_PRINCIPLES §2): the orb is E's signature on Now. The same drawing at the same proportion reads as one product on every screen.
  • It fits G3-1: the confident version is one big, bold thing with quiet things around it. A thin 700 numeral on the biggest orb (Mac) is the weakest of the four.
  • Against B: web is not a lesser surface; a plainer orb makes web the odd one out (DESIGN_PRINCIPLES §2).
  • C (keep as drawn) is also possible: it accepts two looks for the same thing.

Accessibility

  • Contrast: numeral #241F2B on #F1F0FB 14.2:1; “min left” #6A6273 on #F1F0FB 5.2:1. Same in every option.
  • Fit (measured): under A the centre stack is 149 px tall on Mac (inner disc 179) and 127 px on web (inner disc 153).
  • Larger text: the numeral is display-sized and does not grow; “min left” grows. At 200 % the stack still sits inside the inner disc on all four, as drawn and under A (Mac 169 of 179, web 145 of 153, iPhone 144 of 150).
  • Screen readers and targets: unchanged; the whole orb is one button.

Platforms and what is not touched

  • iPhone and Android: unchanged in A and B.
  • Mac and web: only the numeral, icon and label inside the orb change in A. The orb size, ring, arc, track and layout stay.
  • Not touched: the phone Full Today (G3-1), the compact rings (Now mini window, task sheet header) and the E-Minimal Day column.
  • Flagged drawings: the two E-Minimal items awaiting your review (Day review column, web Day menu) are not changed by this decision.

Tablet implications (recorded, not drawn)

  • Minimal on iPad and Android tablets: the orb takes its size from the window (phone size in compact windows, Mac-like in regular); under A its centre type follows the same 0.36 proportion.
  • Two panes and foldables: the orb stays in the Now pane; foldables follow the window class.
  • Round 2: part of the tablet checklist.
