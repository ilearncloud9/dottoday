One month component, three ways in Design-only
Same grid, same data, everywhere
  • Calendar tab (full month), title ▾ sheet (quick jump), pull-down on Today (peek). Built: shared/dot/month_grid.dart and month_data; the tab is new, the other two exist.
  • Monday first, like the built week strip; follows the week-start setting.
  • Dots use the task-dot states at 6–7 px; at most three, then +n. No red, no "late": past days are only slightly lighter.
  • Week wherever the width allows (Q5): iPad and Android tablets get Month · Week in the Calendar view; phones keep Month.
Accessibility
  • Every day is a button: "Tuesday 3 November, today, selected, 9 tasks". Targets 52 × 62 pt (iPhone), 56 × 62 dp (Android).
  • Swipe left or right to change month; previous / next buttons are always there too.
  • Dynamic Type: at large sizes the dots give way to a task count under the number.
Minimal mode
No Calendar tab (ED-068). Tapping the date ▾ opens the same month sheet, as E-PhonesMinimal draws it; Week stays a Full view.
