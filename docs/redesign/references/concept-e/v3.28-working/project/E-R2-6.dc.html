Stage 2, first board (ED-166). Multi-select is built in the app but drawn on no E board (CR-078), so this covers phones and tablets together. R2-6a decided: A (ED-278). Phones from E-Phones; tablets on the approved touch Workspace. Dark on E-R2-6-Dark. Approved by the owner, 13 Nov 2026 (ED-279).

Approved · ED-279 R2-6a approved · A Light
  • Selecting (built, docs/03 §3.2, R-05): hold a dot (iOS) or long-press (Android) to start; then tap rows to add or remove. Each row ends in a ring: empty, or filled with a check — never colour alone. Picked rows get a light teal tint. On the grid, a picked block gets the teal outline and a check badge.
  • Pointer and keyboard: ⇧ / ⌘ / Ctrl-click adds to the selection (built); ↑↓ with Space picks rows; Esc ends selecting.
  • Copy is the built strings only: “3 selected”, “Done selecting”, Move, Duplicate, Colour, Save as routine, Delete; Delete asks “Delete 3 tasks?” (built).
  • Switching Full ↔ Minimal clears a selection without applying anything (ED-100). Mac and web follow the tablet frames with the pointer.
