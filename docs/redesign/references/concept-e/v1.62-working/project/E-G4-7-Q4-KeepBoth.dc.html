Q4i · Keep both unavailable: together over 10,000 characters
Approved (Q4-L1): Keep both is disabled when the whole combined note (both notes, the blank line, the label line and every line break) would pass 10,000 as the app validator counts. Both versions stay readable, scrollable and copyable; Keep mine and Keep theirs stay available; nothing is trimmed, resolved or claimed saved.
Q4-L1 approved (ED-196) · design only · not built
Q4 · rules (ED-195)
Scope: Keep both for notes only. Titles, dates, times, routines, colours and settings keep Keep mine / Keep theirs.
Format: notes are plain text up to 10,000 characters (app and server); conflicts store only the two values and when the conflict was found. Combined text = this device's notes, a blank line, "— From your other device —", the other device's notes. No schema change.
Saving: one ordinary notes edit through the normal save and sync; applied once on repeated taps or retries; if either version changed before the save, back to conflict review; offline or failed save keeps both versions and shows the existing "Couldn't save"; success only after the save.
Undo (Q3): restores this device's notes only if the note is unchanged since; otherwise conflict review. No other field changes.
Accessibility: each version is a labelled group read in full ("This device's notes", "Other device's notes, in use now"); Keep both hint "Adds the other device's notes below yours"; focus order Keep mine, Keep theirs, Keep both; stacked on phones and at 200 %.
Limit (Q4-L1, ED-196): Keep both is disabled with "Keep both isn't available" when the full combined text is over 10,000 counted as Dart String.length (UTF-16 units), the strictest of the app validator, SQLite length() and Postgres char_length (code points); each version has Copy; no trimming.
Ownership: CR-160 isolation applies; not verified yet.
