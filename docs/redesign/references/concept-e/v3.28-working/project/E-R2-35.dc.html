Design-only docs/14 §3 Simplified: before and after only, no per-change labels or rules; Accept plan or Discard
Rules
Nothing moves before Accept
Every trigger opens the plan; Discard keeps the day as it is (CLAUDE.md §11).
Pins and events hold
A pinned task and a calendar event are never moved; the plan works around them.
No shame
Changes say shifted, shrunk or moved, never late or missed. The sheet title is the one open copy question.
Undo after
“Plan accepted” keeps Undo for 30 s.
Android mirrors iPhone
The same sheet and order in Material, as a bottom sheet (ED-338, ED-340).
Text from the app
Every string is built. Tasks and times are examples. No new copy.
