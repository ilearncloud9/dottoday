The First Thread

From opening the app to your own day in under 30 seconds and three taps. You set the two ends of your day, put one real thing on it, and only then is a reminder offered: for that thing. One continuous motion carries the day dot into your thread and your thread into Today. Built on Apple’s and Android’s own guidance and on what made the six options work. Why it works
Value before set-up
People reach their own day in under 30 seconds and three taps (Start, Continue, Put it on today). Everything else is deferred.
Apple: avoid delays before the content; defer non-essential requests. Material “Quickstart”.
Ask in context, never at launch
Notifications are asked the moment they mean something: right after the first task, with that task’s own reminder previewed.
Apple WWDC25 privacy guidance; Android 13 notification permission guidance.
Personal from the first second
Your sun and moon shape the thread; your first task is the first dot. No sample data to unlearn.
Material “Self-Select”: few choices, tied to what the app does.
One continuous motion story
The welcome dot becomes the thread; the thread becomes Today. Every motion explains where you are. Reduce Motion: cross-fades, nothing moves.
HIG Motion: purposeful, brief, optional.
Progressive disclosure
Layout, habits, review and sync are offered later, at the moment they help (the row below).
Teach UI in context, not up front.
Calm, kind copy
No streaks, no “missed”, no pressure. Skip is never punished; every choice is editable.
The app’s no-shame rule (CLAUDE.md §11).

Accessibility and trust
  • VoiceOver / TalkBack: the sun and moon are adjustable sliders (“Wake Up, 7:00 AM, swipe up or down”); the day dot is one image.
  • Text up to 200 %: each screen scrolls; buttons stay reachable at the bottom.
  • Reduce Motion and Remove animations: every move becomes a cross-fade; nothing loops in the app.
  • Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text; colour is never the only signal (icons and words on every dot).
  • Age note (13+) on the first screen; privacy: the first task is parsed on the device; nothing is sent without an account.
  • Returning users: “I have an account” goes straight to sign-in and restore, never through set-up again.
What changes from the built flow
The built flow has seven steps (Welcome, Layout, Day shape, Habits, Reminders, Account, Done). The First Thread keeps Welcome and Day shape (made visual), replaces Habits with one real first task, moves Reminders into context, and moves Layout and Account to the “later” cards. New copy is marked for review. Building it replaces the current flow (no second flow is kept).
