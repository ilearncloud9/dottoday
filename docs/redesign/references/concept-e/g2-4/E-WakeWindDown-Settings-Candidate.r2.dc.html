Recommended integration

  1. Two ends, one night. Keep Dottoday's words: Wake Up starts the day, Wind Down ends it, and the night is the span between. The dial draws the night with the moon and sun caps from the thread, in their own colours.
  2. No separate bedtime. Apple's Wind Down is a run-up before bedtime; ours is the moment the day ends. Adding a bedtime would create two evening times for one idea. If you want a lights-out time later, it would be a new feature (Wind Down + a buffer).
  3. Schedules are a view of Different days. Weekdays and Weekend are groups of weekdays with the same times; Every day covers any day without a schedule. No migration.
  4. One place. Settings › Your day › Wake Up and Wind Down holds schedules, the night (sleep target, night arc), the alarm and its reminders. Today's seven separate Day shape and Wake Up rows move here; the alarm keeps every built option.
  5. Alarm stays a phone feature. Each schedule only says whether it rings; sound and snooze are shared. Mac and web edit the same schedules and say the alarm rings on the phone.
  6. No-shame copy. "Matches / under / over your 8 h sleep target" in plain text, never orange or red, never "short".

Night dial layout rule

  1. Four regions, outside in: hour labels · night band with the moon and sun · ticks · a reserved centre disc. Nothing else may enter the disc.
  2. Only four labels, outside the band: 12 AM / 6 AM / 12 PM / 6 PM (24-hour: 00 / 06 / 12 / 18). Side labels stack ("6" over "AM") so they stay narrow.
  3. Fit test: the duration and "of night" must fit the disc's inscribed box with 6 pt padding. Try 34 → 30 → 28 → 26 pt (times the text scale). Never smaller than 26 pt.
  4. If it does not fit, it moves below the dial at full size and the disc shows only a moon. Always below at text scale 135% or more, or when the dial is under 240 pt.
  5. Size: the dial is the card width minus 32 pt, from 240 pt (iPhone SE) to 360 pt (tablets); the band is 12% of the size, the handles band + 4 pt (44 pt targets on phones).
  6. Readable without the dial: the two time fields under it always show both times; VoiceOver and TalkBack read "Wind Down 11:00 PM, Wake Up 6:30 AM, 7 h 30 of night".
The same rule applies anywhere a 24-hour ring with a centre value is drawn (for example the Wake Up card and onboarding).

Feasibility and recommendation

What the code does today (audited)
  • Every iOS and Android install with Ring like an alarm on arms its own alarm: iPad counts as iPhone and an Android tablet as a phone (health_providers.dart, isPhone). The alarm setting is synced, so two devices on one account can both ring today.
  • Mac and web already never arm (AlarmBlocker.notAPhone).
  • One device rings each Wake Up once across the alarm and notification paths (R-10, wake_up_arming.dart); nothing coordinates across devices. There is no device list in sync (no table in supabase/migrations).
What the design needs
  • A synced device list (id, model, editable name, platform, alarm tier, last seen, armed schedule version) and an account setting alarm device. New Supabase table with RLS: a schema change you approve before any push.
  • One arming rule: a device arms Wake Up only when it is the alarm device and Ring like an alarm is on; every other device disarms on its next sync. Snooze, I'm up and missions stay on the ringing device (built).
  • iPad: AlarmKit is iOS and iPadOS 26 (verify on a real iPad at build time). Android tablets: same exact alarm + full-screen path; Play's full-screen-intent policy must accept Dottoday as an alarm app (verify).
  • Device names: iOS 16+ returns only "iPhone"/"iPad" without Apple's user-assigned-device-name entitlement, so the list shows the model plus a name you can edit. Android exposes the device name.
  • Restart, time zone, DST: built: Android re-arms on boot; AlarmKit alarms persist; civil times re-resolve on time-zone change (doc 15 §3).
Limits we state, not hide
  • No exactly-once guarantee across devices: during a switch, an offline old device can ring once. The design says so in the switch message.
  • Changes reach the ringing device only when it syncs. A silent push (APNs / FCM) can speed that up; iOS doesn't guarantee background delivery, so "Waiting to sync" stays visible until it confirms.
Recommendation
Adopt one alarm device per account with the arming rule above; default it to the device where you turn the alarm on. Fix today's double ring with the same rule. Backup ringing is not proposed: it would bring back duplicate alarms. If you want it later, it could be an opt-in "If my iPhone hasn't confirmed by Wind Down, ring here instead", and it would need its own approval.
