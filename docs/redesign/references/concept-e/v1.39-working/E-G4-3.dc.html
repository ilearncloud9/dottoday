G4-3 · Mac minimum window
Today: the app opens at 1280 × 800 and never shrinks below 960 × 640 (owner-approved after the M4 sweep, MainFlutterWindow.swift). Every Mac design is drawn at 1280 × 800.
Against the new rules: 960 is expanded under 600 / 840 (the register's old worry that 960 is "medium" came from the superseded 720 / 1160 proposal). The editor floats there (below 1160). Height 640 keeps H1 unreachable on Mac, so a Mac window never gets compact navigation.
Real gaps: (1) E-Minimal's Now + Day column at less than 1280 is not drawn; (2) at 960 a window cannot be tiled to half of a 13"–16" laptop screen.
Approved (ED-169): B, 840 × 640, design only; MainFlutterWindow.swift is unchanged until implementation is authorised. Narrow designs and the Day-column fold proposal: board E-G4-3-Narrow.
