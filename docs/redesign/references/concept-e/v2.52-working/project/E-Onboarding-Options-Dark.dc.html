E · Onboarding · six new directions

Your request: a creative redesign of the first thing people see, with a different flow from today’s, crisp, modern and with motion. Six options for you to compare (D, E and F added at your request). iPhone shown at 50 %. Light on E-Onboarding-Options.

Options · for owner decision Dark
  • New proposals, not decisions. R2-16 (the built flow drawn in E) is unchanged on its own board. Pick one, mix parts, or ask for changes; nothing is built until you approve.
  • All three keep what the app needs: the 13+ age note on the first screen, Wake Up and Wind Down, an optional account, and the Full / Minimal choice (asked or deferred). New copy is marked for your review.
  • Motion plays on this board (loops). Every motion stops under Reduce Motion and is replaced by the end state; nothing flashes; durations 200–600 ms in the app.
  • Other widths: Android uses the same screens with its own controls; tablets, Mac and web show them in a centred column, as on R2-16.
A · Draw your day
4 screens · ~45 sMotion: the thread grows
One living screen. Each answer becomes a dot, and you watch your own thread draw itself.
Wake Up→Habits land→Wind Down closes it→Ready
  • Motion: the dotted thread grows down from Wake Up; habit chips arc onto it and pop into dots with a spring; the ring closes into the day dot.
  • Changes from today: no Layout or Reminders steps up front; both become quiet choices on the last screen (“Ask later”, “Show me less”). Account is optional at the end.
  • Feels like: building, not filling in a form.
B · Try it first
5 screens · learn by doingMotion: coach and sweep
No questions first. You land on a sample day and learn the three moves that matter, then make it yours.
Hello→Finish a dot→Drag→Type a task→Make it yours
  • Motion: the target pulses; the dot fills with its colour as you tap; a hand shows the drag; your typing appears; the sample sweeps away.
  • Changes from today: the habits and layout steps go; you learn Done, drag and quick add (R-04, R-06) before any set-up. Only Wake Up and Wind Down are asked.
  • Feels like: the app already works for you in 30 seconds.
C · Pick your rhythm
4 screens · bold and visualMotion: ring, morph, live nudge
Big, swipeable choices instead of forms: your rhythm as a 24-hour ring, the layout as a live morph, one real-looking nudge.
Rhythm→How much to see→Nudge→Welcome
  • Motion: the 24-hour ring turns as you swipe cards; the preview morphs between Everything and Less; a real notification slides in; the ring shrinks into the day dot.
  • Changes from today: day shape becomes four rhythm cards (with “My own” for exact times); Layout shows a live preview; habits move to the first Today as suggestions.
  • Feels like: choosing, not configuring.
D · Say your day
4 screens · voice or textMotion: waveform, words become dots
Tell it your day in one sentence. The words light up, become chips, then a proposed thread you accept.
Tell me→I heard→Is this your day?→Drawn
  • Motion: a live waveform while you speak; the times and tasks in your sentence highlight one by one and rise as chips; proposed dots are dashed until you say yes.
  • Rules kept: it is a proposal (ProposalSheet rule: nothing saved before “Yes”); the sentence is read on the device by the built parser, nothing is sent off it. Spoken input needs the system’s on-device speech; typing always works.
  • Feels like: talking to a planner, not setting one up.
E · Sunrise, sunset
4 screens · immersiveMotion: the sky changes with you
Set your day on a living sky: drag the sun up for Wake Up, the moon down for Wind Down; the horizon becomes your thread.
Good morning→Sunrise→Sunset→Your day
  • Motion: the sky gradient shifts from night to day as the sun rises; stars appear at dusk; the horizon folds into the vertical thread with the two end dots.
  • Changes from today: Wake Up and Wind Down are the whole set-up; habits, nudges and layout are asked in context later (first Today, first task start, Settings).
  • Feels like: calm and memorable, a strong first impression.
F · Swipe to set up
5 cards · ~20 sMotion: cards fling, progress fills
A deck of five yes / skip cards with sensible defaults. Swipe right to keep, left to skip; a summary at the end.
Hello card→Day→Habits→Nudges→Layout→All set
  • Motion: each card flings off with a tilt; the next rises; the top progress bar fills; buttons mirror the swipe for anyone who can’t swipe (and for screen readers).
  • Changes from today: every step becomes one default you accept or skip; nothing to type. The last card summarises, and all of it stays editable in Settings.
  • Feels like: fast and playful; the shortest path to Today.
