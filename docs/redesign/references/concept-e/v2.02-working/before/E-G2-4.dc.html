Part 1 · the Settings root
A (recommended). Six groups (2), with Layout as the first row and Account on top; Mac panes start with General (4); minimal keeps its nine rows (3) and Everything else… opens this root.
B. Keep today's nine sections (1); only the entry point moves (G2-3, adopted).
C. Six groups, and minimal Settings also becomes the six groups (no nine-row page).
Nothing is removed in any option: every built row keeps a home and a search hit.
Part 2 · what "Everything else" means
A (recommended). "Everything else…" is the Settings row (built). E-Minimal's finder keeps its design and is called "Search or run" on ⌘K / k (6), like Full's palette (7).
B. Keep both names as drawn: the wide finder and the phone Settings row are both "Everything else".
C. "Everything else" becomes the finder everywhere, phones included, and the Settings row goes. This removes a built entry point.
What changes on your boards if you choose A + A
  • E-Minimal: the finder's title and accessible name become "Search or run" (two words; layout untouched).
  • No Settings screen is drawn on any E board yet: the root (2), minimal page (3) and Mac window (4) would be added to E-Phones, E-PhonesMinimal and E-FullA-v2 as new frames, shown to you first.
  • Phones, tablets, web: Settings search is new design; every row it finds is built.
