Rules
Proposals before writes
Break it down is the one flow here that proposes: nothing is written before Accept (CLAUDE.md §11). Discard, ×, swipe down or Esc write nothing.
Undo for the quiet ones
Unstick writes its one step with Undo; Smallest step only opens Focus; the reality insight writes nothing.
On this device
The model runs on the device or not at all; what you type never leaves it (ADR-0009). No model means your history and simple rules.
No shame
Smallest step and Unstick use the accent, never red; “not started yet”, never late or missed.
Same everywhere (R-13)
Every flow on every platform; Mac and web show proposals as a side panel.
Text from the app
Every string on this board is built (app_en.arb). Tasks, times and counts are examples. No new copy is proposed.
