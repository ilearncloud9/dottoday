Rules for every reminder
Exactly once (R-10)
An alert’s ID is its occurrence and its slot; re-planning cancels stale ones first. Up to 5 alerts per task; a snooze replaces the ones before it with one reminder.
Calm words
Title over when: “Starts in 10 minutes”, “Starting now”, “Ending now”, “Ended 15 minutes ago. Done?”. No late, missed or overdue; no red anywhere (no-shame rule).
Nothing is lost
Quiet hours, Focus and Do Not Disturb make reminders silent, never gone. Health says what is in the way, in plain words, with one fix each.
Same features everywhere (R-13)
iPhone, Android, iPad, Mac and web all get alerts, snooze, Wind Down, the summary, health and the test alarm. The web snoozes from the strip.
System chrome is the system’s
Notifications are drawn by iOS, Android, macOS and the browser. Dottoday sets the title, text, actions, channel and sound only.
Text from the app
Every string on this board is built (app_en.arb). No new copy is proposed.
