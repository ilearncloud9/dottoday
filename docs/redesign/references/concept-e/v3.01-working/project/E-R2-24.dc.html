What the pass found
Needs a string The Calendar tab (ED-069, ED-075) has no built label: it shows English in every language. A new key with German and French text is new copy, for your OK
Wraps French “Boîte de réception” cannot fit the tab at 10 pt: two lines, or the shorter “Boîte” as a tab-only string (new copy). “Aujourd’hui” just fits the selected pill
Fits The four sheet buttons wrap to two lines in German and French (“Rest verschieben”, “Décaler la suite”, “Concentration”) inside the 56 pt tile, as built
Fits Accept, Discard and the footnote; the carry buttons wrap to a second line in French; toasts grow to two lines
Flag German and French release notes (“Tagesform”, “Forme de la journée”) and the move answer (“Rändern”, “bords”) carry the same out-of-date wording as English
Rules
Proposals before writes
Place them (carry to next week) is a proposal: nothing moves before you accept. Leave in Inbox and Disconnect ask or offer Undo.
No shame
“No streaks here — just what happened.” The week is never compared with another week, and unfinished is never red.
Yours to share
The share image shows no titles unless you switch them on; feedback never includes what your tasks say.
On this device
App lock and Help with thinking are per device; calendar access is asked once.
Same everywhere (R-13)
Every screen on every platform; the web has no device calendars or lock switch, and says so.
Text from the app
Every string is built (app_en, app_de, app_fr). Three flags: the Calendar tab has no string, and two built texts are out of date.
