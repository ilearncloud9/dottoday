      How every surface behaves
      
    
      
      
      One snapshot, five intentsSurfaces read the day snapshot and run only Add task, Mark done, +15 min, Start focus and Open. None talks to Supabase (CLAUDE.md §11).
      
      
      The thread and the dotThe task under way is a ring in its colour that fills as time passes. Dots ahead are hollow, done ones are filled. The day dot fills from Wake Up to Wind Down.
      
      
      Text from the appEvery word arrives formatted and translated in the snapshot: Now, Next up, until {time}, {time} left, Done, +15 min, {n} in Inbox. No new strings.
      
      
      Act without openingDone and +15 min run in place on widgets, the Live Activity, the island, the notification and the menu. Add task and Start focus open the app where they must.
      
      
      Calm by defaultNo overdue badges, no red, no streaks. A task that ran past its end just stops being “now”. The notification is silent and alerts once.
      
      
      Native chrome, one identityWidgetKit and Glance shapes, the Dynamic Island, Quick Settings, the macOS menu and the browser tab keep their system form; the dot and colours are Dottoday’s.
