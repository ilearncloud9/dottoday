4 · Accessibility and behaviour
Findings, corrections and theme behaviour
Passes (P1): text ≥ 10.8:1 and secondary ≥ 7.0:1 on every surface; teal ≥ 6.0:1; control outline ≥ 3.45:1; all 12 task rings ≥ 3.02:1 on every surface; glyphs and checks inside filled dots ≥ 4.37:1; error, warning and success text ≥ 5.4:1.
Needs correction: (1) iOS switch: the approved teal #11615C is 2.1:1 on dark rows → dark on-tint #2A8C83 (3.8:1, white thumb 4.1:1); light mode keeps #11615C (ED-112). (2) The focus ring is only 1.2:1 against a teal button → always drawn with a 2 px gap of surface. (3) Five task colours (indigo, violet, slate, brown, plum) are lightened for dark; their identity is kept (same hue).
Decorative / exempt: dividers (≈ 1.3–1.6:1), the now track, and disabled text (≥ 3.45:1). Selected tabs also change icon and text colour, not only background (tonal vs surface 1.4:1).
Theme behaviour: Appearance Light · Dark · Auto (ED-161); Auto follows the platform setting. Switching theme never loses unsaved edits, changes the task or date, commits a proposal, resets navigation or recreates an open editor. Full and Minimal unchanged. Tablets record the same tokens; layouts in Round 2.
