How they differ
A · The threadB · The day dot
Leads withThe list of what is next, on a threadThe shape of the whole day, then the countdown
Best atReading several tasks at once (medium and large widgets, the Mac menu)A one-second glance (small widgets, the island, the Lock Screen, the favicon)
Closest toToday in the app (approved E thread)The day dot (the brand mark) and the R2-16 welcome ring
Small sizesA ring for now plus text; the list needs roomThe ring alone still says how much of the day is gone
Native workText-led: easiest in WidgetKit and GlanceDrawn rings: SwiftUI paths on iOS; a bitmap on Android (DayDotBitmap.kt exists)
AccessibilityAlready list-shaped for VoiceOver and TalkBackThe ring is one image with a spoken summary (“3 of 10 done, 18 min left”)
Recommendation
B for the small, glance-first surfaces (small widgets, the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island, the menu-bar glyph and the favicon), A for the reading surfaces (medium and large Today widgets and the Mac menu). If one option must cover everything, A is the safer pick: it matches Today and costs the least native work. Your call: A, B or the mix.
