The thread wakes up

A second direction for the Wake Up alarm. Instead of a briefing card, the morning is the thread itself: Android’s ringing screen is the dawn round the day dot, and “I’m up” opens your real Today thread starting at Wake Up, with the first dot ready and any shift shown on the line. Option A is on E-R2-19. Dark on E-R2-19-B-Dark.

The same rule applies on any background the ring sits on, in both themes.
The other missions
Stand up is the default when missions are turned on; each one is opt-in, chosen in Settings › Wake Up, and every one has Can’t right now (hold 5 s). On the board each loops its own motion; Reduce Motion shows a still mid-state. Rings and dots use the App colour (ring rule).
