Why A

  • Accessibility: the teal track is 7.16:1 against its row; system green is 2.19:1, below the 3:1 that controls need.
  • One accent: teal marks selected and active states across E (§4.2); green would add a second “on” colour on iPhone only.
  • Still native: it is the system switch with an app tint, which iOS supports; shape, motion and haptics stay Apple's.
  • Already drawn: A is what all approved boards show, so choosing it changes no board.
  • Against A: iOS users are used to green; B would match other apps' Settings exactly.

Platforms and tablets

  • iPhone: as chosen. iPad: the same switch and colour (recorded for Round 2; no tablet drawing).
  • Android phones and tablets: M3 switch in teal, unchanged.
  • Mac and web: toggles drawn off only; not part of G3-4.
  • Dark mode: follows G4-6.
