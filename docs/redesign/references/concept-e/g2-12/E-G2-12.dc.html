Per platform (option A)

  • iPhone: sheets with detents; Week in review pushes inside the review sheet.
  • Android: M3 bottom sheets; predictive Back closes them first; M3 chip shapes.
  • Mac: Review is a Workspace place (Full) or the right column (minimal); the picker is a popover with typed date and time fields.
  • Web: same as Mac; /review keeps its address; Esc closes the popover.
  • Accessibility: the picker is the non-drag route (R-04, R-12): the thread is a slider with text values; suggestions are buttons with full times.

Tablet implications (recorded, not drawn)

  • iPad: the review as a pane beside the day in landscape, a sheet in narrow Split View; the picker as a popover from the row.
  • Android tablets and foldables: review in the supporting pane in expanded; picker as a side sheet; a drop from the Inbox still places directly.
  • Round 2: part of the tablet checklist; no tablet frames here.
