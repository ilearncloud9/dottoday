Strengths

  • Lowest risk to "the timeline is the product": painters, gestures, providers and routes are reused.
  • The same thread on all four platforms; only the shell changes.
  • The ⋯ catch-all becomes one grouped Day menu, and every former ⋯ item has a named home.
  • Add is visible at every width; Brain dump and Routine are 1 tap from Add.
  • Move works, and doubles as the screen-reader "Move to…".

Problems

  • The least different concept; it can read as "the same app, polished".
  • The Day menu is still a menu: Pick one thing and Low-energy stay one level deep.
  • Running late is 3 taps on phones, 1–2 on wide windows.
  • Wide windows stay a day thread, not a planning workspace.

Accessibility concerns

  • The medium-detent sheet must work at 200 % text: the four action tiles re-stack to two rows.
  • Halos and the dotted thread are decorative; state is read from text ("Now", "done") and the ring, never colour alone.
  • Under reduced motion the bead glow is static and the completion pulse becomes a fade.
  • Day menu sections need announced headers; Running late on another day needs a spoken reason.
  • Dimmed side days keep text at 4.5:1 or better.

Engineering tradeoffs

  • Rebuilt: shells, the task surface, Settings, the Day menu and missions. Reused: the timeline itself.
  • iOS glass and Mac vibrancy are approximated in Flutter (blur + tint); the native look needs a platform view.
  • Web path URLs need a static-host rewrite; ?task= makes the inspector restorable.
  • One editor must work at the medium detent, the large detent and as an inspector.
  • SF Symbols chrome needs a native path; Material Symbols on Android do not.

Hypotheses (not approved)

  • Settings leaves the tab bar: avatar, sidebar footer, ⌘,.
  • One task surface: sheet, /task/:id and editor merge; every entry point kept.
  • Calendar becomes a destination (Month on phones, Week + Month wide).
  • Brain dump and Routine become modes of Add.
  • Day menu items work on any viewed day; Running late stays today-only.
  • Settings regrouped into six jobs, duplicates merged.
