Rules carried over from D2

  • Swipe down or Done closes; never discards (edits are already saved).
  • Repeating tasks: the first change asks "This one or all future?" once per open, never per field.
  • Delete is the same everywhere: moves to Trash at once, Undo in the message and ⌘Z (built Trash keeps 30 days).
  • Large text: opens straight at the large detent; tiles wrap 2 × 2; nothing truncates.
  • Android: M3 sheet expands to full screen; predictive Back steps full → partial → Today.
  • Mac and web: the inspector already has this content in this order (E-FullA-v2); iPad uses a popover from the dot, the inspector when wide.
  • Kept: /task/:id, dottoday:// links, notification taps, Search results, the selection bar, Break it down and More (⋯ in the header).
