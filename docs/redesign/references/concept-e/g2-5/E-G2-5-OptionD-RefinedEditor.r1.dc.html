Option C and option D

Same in both
  • The task sheet grows to full height; Done closes; edits save as you go (built autosave) with Undo.
  • Header dot, title and time; Done · Move · Shift the rest · Focus tiles; Need more time?
  • Every field stays, nothing removed; /task/:id and other entry points unchanged.
What D changes
  • The task dot is also the Colour and icon button (palette badge); a tinted header in the task colour.
  • Date, time and alerts in one icon card; Repeat and Energy as two pills; Sound, Pin, Travel and Delete in a More card and in ⋯.
  • Steps edited in place (checkbox + text, ↩ adds the next, × removes) with Notes under them in the same card.
  • Colour and icon: a compact sheet (palette, current, suggested) that drags up into the full library with a sticky glass palette.
  • A full-sheet scope chooser for repeating tasks instead of a small alert; a Create task variant of the same editor.
Easier with D
  • Changing colour or icon: one tap on the dot, no hunting for a row.
  • Writing steps and notes: typed in place, no extra screens.
  • Reading the task: the most-used fields first, fewer rows.
Could get harder with D
  • The tinted header and palette badge add visual weight to a calm screen.
  • Sound, Pin and Travel move behind ⋯ / More: one more step.
  • Two layers of sheets (editor, then picker) to manage on small phones.
Redesigns of built behaviour
  • Colour and icon picker (9 E colours; 525 icons, 16 categories, search, up to 2 suggested — built).
  • Repeat scopes: This one / This and following / All — built (domain/recurrence/series_edit.dart). "All" never changes past ones (built rule); updating past tasks is not offered.
  • Steps per occurrence (R-09) and the parent's "2 of 4" — built. Notes with links and formatting — built.
Needs your approval or a check
  • New: Recently used icons (not in the picker today).
  • New: scope asked before saving a repeating change (today the editor asks; autosave must hold the change until you choose) — needs an implementation check.
  • Free/Pro: no icon is locked today (isPro is true until you sign off a split), so D shows no locks. A future split is out of scope.
  • Glass palette over a scrolling grid: approximated; native material needs a feasibility check; contrast of the selected ring on glass to verify.
  • Calendar series offer only This one and All (built); D follows that.
