E · Round 2 · R2-2 · Today on expanded tablets: the Workspace at touch sizes

Full on expanded tablets is the Workspace (Q1, ED-252) with the touch content scale (Q3, ED-256), inside the approved R2-1 navigation (ED-265, ED-267). Sample day and copy from E-FullA-v2. Frames at 75 %. Dark on E-R2-2-Dark. Approved by the owner, 13 Nov 2026 (ED-269).

Approved · ED-269 Light
  • What changes from Mac: only content size and spacing (ED-256): task titles 17, times 13, the current task’s ring 45 with “Now, 18 min left”, other dots 36 with their icons, Plan-panel rows 60 tall, every control 44 pt / 48 dp. Same parts in the same places: sidebar, Plan panel, toolbar, grid with a thread per day, now line, overlap of Deep work and Call with Sam.
  • Week shows the days that fit: as on Mac (the 1160 window shows Monday to Thursday), the week scrolls sideways and shows as many days as keep a column of at least 180. iPad 1180 shows 3 (Tue–Thu, scrolled to today); Android 1280 shows 4 (Mon–Thu). The Plan panel stays while 3 or more days fit (ED-184).
  • The inspector floats here: at touch sizes, three days plus the inspector (248) do not fit beside the Plan panel, so tapping a block opens the inspector over the grid instead of docking (ED-167, ED-170). Shown open on R2-4.
  • Short tasks (under 35 minutes, e.g. the 15-minute standup, the 30-minute call and dentist) are one-line pills, title 15 with a 24 or 28 dot. A 30-minute pill is 44 tall; a 15-minute pill’s tap area reaches 44 pt because no other block sits within reach (WCAG 2.5.8 spacing).
  • New values for your review (nothing approved draws a touch grid): 96 px per hour, 180 minimum column, block title 17 / pill 15 under 35 minutes, dots 36 / 45 / 28 / 24.
  • Not on this board: dragging (touch long-press is stage 2), the Mac drag from Inbox and the right-click menu (removed), Minimal (drawn on R2-1 at touch sizes), and medium windows (R2-1: the Today list; the editor floats there, R2-4).
