E · Tablet Q2 · the Day menu in Minimal on tablets (iPad and Android)

Decided: A (ED-254). B is kept as history. Both rows use the approved Minimal window (E-G4-3-Narrow 1160 on iPad, 1280 on Android) with only the Mac title bar removed, and the approved menus (E-Minimal’s Day menu; E-PhonesMinimal’s menu), opened from the Day button. At 60 %. Nothing is applied to an active board.

Approved · A (ED-254, owner, 13 Nov) Light only · approved (ED-254)
A · By width, as on Mac and web
Approved · ED-254E-Minimal’s menu at medium and expandedPhone menu in compact windows
  • Medium and expanded tablet windows use E-Minimal’s wide layout, so they get its Day menu: Need more time (+5 / +15 / +30), Shift the rest, Brain dump, Drop a routine, Duplicate, Copy as text, Review the day, Low-energy mode, Show everything (ED-093).
  • Compact tablet windows (Split View ⅓, Slide Over) use the phone layout and its three-item menu.
  • Same rule as Q1 (ED-252) and CR-093: width decides, never the device.
  • iPad: native pull-down (UIMenu) from Day ▾. Android: M3 menu anchored to the Day button.
