What this shows, and what it does not

  • Tint keeps vibrancy: the 35 % warm layer leaves about 60 % of the native show-through and improves readability; the material is still genuine.
  • Readability: every option passes 4.5 : 1 for body text and the selected teal, at the worst point over the stripes.
  • Not tested: dark mode (warm dark tint waits for G4-6), Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast (your Mac's settings not changed), VoiceOver, keyboard Tab traversal, other wallpapers and window behind.
  • Spike only: isolated sample app; not Dottoday.

What any F2 run checks

  • Geometry: bar 21 / 6 / 21, 63 tall, + 63, Day 16 above and right 21; centres level.
  • Tabs switch; Inbox badge in sync; + tap opens Add; hold shows Brain dump, Routine, Paste a list.
  • Day UIMenu opens on a tap, in the fixed G2-9 order.
  • Bar items centred despite the bar's bottom safe-area inset.
  • Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast; VoiceOver order; largest text.
  • Result per version recorded as PASS / FAIL / PENDING; a failure brings alternatives (e.g. F1 Flutter-drawn row) before deciding.
