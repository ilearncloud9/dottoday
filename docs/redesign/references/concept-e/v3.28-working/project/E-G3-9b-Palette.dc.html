What the original app does (read-only audit of dottoday/app, identical in dottoday-redesign/app)
  • 9 task colours, picker order coral · amber · mint · teal · sky · indigo · violet · rose · slate (assets/design/palette.json → TaskColor).
  • Stored as color_key text (≤ 24 chars, default indigo) on tasks and series (Supabase 0001_init.sql); unknown keys render indigo.
  • Legacy keys from the old 12-colour palette still render: tangerine → amber, lime → mint, magenta → rose (never written).
  • No custom colours: the set is a fixed enum.
  • Built dots: fill = colour at 12 % tint, icon = colour deepened to 3.1:1, done check white.
  • Accent (Settings › Look): 4 user-selectable accents indigo #3B6CF6 (default) · teal #1F9E9E · coral #E5674F · violet #7A5AF8, each with a dark value. Concept E fixes the accent at deep teal #11615C and lists "accent" under Appearance without drawing it.
  • Concept E boards draw C's 9 colours (table): same keys re-tinted, except Brown, which has no key.

5 · Rules, custom colours, dark mode, decisions

Accessibility rules for every task colour (G3-9 principle, approved)
  • Fill stays the user's colour. Ring (ahead dot and its icon) = the fill if it reaches 3:1 on every surface it sits on (card #FFFDF9, cream #FBF7F1, Minimal #F3EEE6); otherwise the fill darkened in HSL lightness only until it does (hue and saturation kept).
  • Glyph on a filled dot: white when white reaches 3:1 on the fill, else ink #241F2B.
  • Now: unchanged design (progress arc in the fill + "Now" text); state never by colour alone (hollow / filled + check / arc + text).
  • No outlines, halos or glows added. Faded, scrimmed and preview contexts keep the CR-133 flags.
Custom colours: recommendation not now
  • Not supported today (fixed key set). Adding them needs a new stored value (e.g. color_hex) and a Supabase migration: a separate, must-ask decision.
  • If approved later: the saved hex never changes; ring and glyph are derived at render time by the two rules on the left, for light and (after G4-6) dark; picks too close to the surface get a derived ring, never a rejected colour.
Dark mode (G4-6, not chosen here)
Every family needs a dark fill, ring and glyph check: coral, amber, mint, teal, sky, indigo, violet, rose, slate, and brown, plum, olive if approved; plus the 4 accents (the app already has dark accent values).
What needs your approval
  1. Concept E shades for the 8 shared keys (coral … rose) as drawn, keys unchanged.
  2. Slate: keep the key with E shade #6E6A75, and Brown as a new key (recommended), or re-tint slate as brown.
  3. PROPOSED new colours Plum #9E4F8F and Olive #7F8A3A (12 total), or none.
  4. PROPOSED rings for teal #289588 and rose #D75E89 (Minimal surface).
  5. App colour: E shades for the 4 accent keys, teal (#11615C) as the E default.
  6. Custom colours: not now.
