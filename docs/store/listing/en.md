### Short description
A calm visual day planner: your day as dots on one timeline. Free, no ads.

### Description
Dottoday turns your to-do list into a day you can follow. Your day is a thread from Wake Up to Wind Down, and every task is a dot on it: outlined while it is coming up, filling while it is happening, solid when it is done. One look tells you what is now and what is next.

Starts your morning, ends your evening. Wake Up can ring like an alarm and opens a card with your day; Wind Down moves itself from tomorrow's first task.

WHEN THE DAY MOVES, MOVE IT ALL
• Drag a task to move it, drag its edge to change how long it takes.
• Shift everything after a task in one gesture, or add 15 minutes to what you are doing now.
• Long-press to pick several tasks and move them together.
• Pinned tasks stay put when others shift.

ADD IN SECONDS
• Type "Go shopping 5pm 1h" and Dottoday fills in the time and length.
• Paste a list and each line becomes a task.
• Not sure when? Put it in the Inbox and drop it on a free slot later.

EVERYTHING IMPORTANT IS FREE
• Repeating tasks, with subtasks that reset for every day.
• Your calendars on the same thread: Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Outlook, with changes sent back if you want.
• Reminders at the start or minutes before, several per task, delivered once.
• Export your tasks as JSON, CSV or a calendar file whenever you like.

CALM BY DESIGN
• No scores to keep up, no red marks, no guilt. What you did not get to simply moves with you.
• A month view, and a week board on bigger screens, to see where the time goes.
• Home-screen widgets for what is now and next.

PRIVATE AND YOURS
• Works fully offline, with tasks stored encrypted on your device.
• No account needed. Sign in only if you want sync across your devices.
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers.

FOR EVERYONE
• VoiceOver and TalkBack, large text, reduced motion and full keyboard control on Mac and the web.
• The same app on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and the web.

Dottoday is for people aged 13 and over.
