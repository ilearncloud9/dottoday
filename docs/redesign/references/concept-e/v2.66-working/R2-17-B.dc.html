Rules for this option
The day ring
Wake Up at the top, round the clock to Wind Down. Each task is an arc in its colour: solid when done, pale ahead, wider while it runs. A small dot marks now.
One number to read
The task under way shows minutes left in the middle of its ring. Everything else is a glance at the ring.
Colour is never alone
Every arc also has its title and time nearby, and the countdown is words and numbers; the ring is drawn larger where it carries meaning.
One snapshot, five intents
Every surface reads DaySnapshot and runs only Add task, Mark done, +15 min, Start focus and Open. None talks to Supabase (CLAUDE.md §11).
Text from the app
Only built strings: Now, Next up, Today, until {time}, {time} left, Done, +15 min, {n} in Inbox, Add task, Start focus. No new copy.
No new targets
Everything drawn exists today. A watch app, Mac widgets or a Wake Up Live Activity redesign would be new targets and need the owner first.
