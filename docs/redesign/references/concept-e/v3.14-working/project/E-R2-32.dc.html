Rules
Calm and clear
Delete now and Empty Trash ask once and say plainly that it can’t be undone; the button is ink, never red.
Only on Accept
Nothing is deleted before the confirm; Restore stays one tap away until then.
Now first
Today opens where you are in the day, never under the floating controls.
Say which
Two settings never share one label; screen readers and pickers say which one they change.
Tablets stay put
Missions that ask you to move stay on the phone.
Copy
New copy is underlined amber on this board and needs the owner’s review; the rest is built.
