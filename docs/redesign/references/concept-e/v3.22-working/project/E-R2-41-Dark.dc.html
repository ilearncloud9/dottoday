Rules
Short height keeps the phone
Under 480 of usable height, a phone in landscape keeps its compact layout and its own navigation (ED-168 H1). It never becomes the Workspace and never docks the editor.
A readable column
The day, the editor and Add sit in a 640 column, centred between the safe areas. Rows put the time beside the title, as built.
Less chrome, same parts
The week strip folds into the title row; tab bar items sit side by side. Nothing is removed: the month is a tap on the title.
Nothing resets
Turning the phone, or opening the keyboard, never saves, discards, closes a sheet or changes Full or Minimal. Text from the app; no new copy.
