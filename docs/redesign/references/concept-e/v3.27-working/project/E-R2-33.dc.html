Siri & Shortcuts
Siri can add a task, tell you what's next, and help with the task you're on — without opening Dottoday.
Say “Hey Siri” or hold the side button, then say a phrase.
What you can say
“Add a task in Dottoday”Siri asks what to add. Say a time and it goes on your day; otherwise it goes to the Inbox.
“What's next in Dottoday”Siri says what's on now, or what comes next.
“Mark my task done in Dottoday”Ticks off the task you're on.
“Give my task 15 more minutes in Dottoday”Gives the task you're on 15 more minutes.
“Start focus in Dottoday”Opens Focus on the task you're on.
Shortcuts
Open Shortcuts
The same five actions are in the Shortcuts app, ready for the Action button, Control Center or your Home Screen.

Rules
As built
Every row, order and string is the app’s; only the page frame is the approved Settings design.
Icons
Each row has its one icon and colour on every platform (ED-332).
Hear it first
Tapping a sound plays it; nothing changes until you pick.
No shame
“Running-late nudge” is the one string that breaks the rule; it is flagged for your word.
Parity (R-13)
Planning and Sounds are the same everywhere; Siri is iPhone-only by nature, with Android’s shortcuts as its counterpart.
Text from the app
Every string is built. Values are examples. No new copy.
