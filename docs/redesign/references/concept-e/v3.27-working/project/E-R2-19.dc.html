Rules for the morning
One alarm device
Only the chosen phone or tablet rings (ED-083). Mac and web never ring and say “Wake-up alarms ring on your phone.”
No shame
No streaks, no “late”: “You’re up 25 min after plan.” and a choice. Skipping a mission is never counted on screen.
Nothing changes before you accept
Shift the rest? always goes through the ProposalSheet. Start the day starts Focus on the first dot or marks the day started.
Stops reliably
Stops on I’m up, a finished mission, the 10-minute cap, sign-out, or when its day goes. One alarm a day, plus at most one check.
Text from the app
Every string is built: Wake Up, Good morning. {date}., First: {title} at {time}, I’m up, Snooze, Start the day, Later, Shift the rest?, Start {title}?, Tap to start.
Motion
The card rises over Today (400 ms, emphasized decelerate); the day dot draws once. Reduce Motion: cross-fades. The ringing screen itself never animates.
