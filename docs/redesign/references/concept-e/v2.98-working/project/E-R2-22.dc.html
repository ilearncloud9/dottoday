4 · Day shape
Day shape: decided, the row is removed (ED-321)
On 14 Nov 2026 you chose A. The Day shape row (“Morning, focus and evening blocks”) is gone from Settings › Your day on every board that draws it: E-Phones and E-R2-11, light and dark. The Your day summary on the Settings root now reads “Wake Up and Wind Down, learning” on every board that shows it.
  • Nothing is lost: Wake Up, Wind Down, different days, the sleep target and the night live in Wake Up and Wind Down (ED-084, R2-18).
  • Week starts and Time format live in Appearance.
  • Morning, focus and evening blocks are not planned; there is no new copy, data or spec.
Rules
Proposals before writes
A routine drop and “Move everything optional to tomorrow?” are proposals: nothing is written before Add to day or Move them (CLAUDE.md §11).
Undo everywhere else
Duplicate day, saving, editing and deleting a routine all write once, with Undo.
Calm, no counts
Low-energy mode hides counts and pauses nudges until the next Wake Up; Not today never says late or missed.
Same everywhere (R-13)
Routines, Duplicate day, Copy as text and Low-energy mode on every platform; tablets, Mac and web reach Routines from the sidebar.
Free (R-01)
Routines are recurring work: free, as every non-negotiable is.
Text from the app
Every string on this board is built (app_en.arb). The routines and times are examples. No new copy is proposed.
