What the code does today, and what is decided already

  • Built: the task editor already autosaves (debounced DraftAutosave, "nothing lost on dismiss", doc 03 §3.4) and also shows Save and Cancel (editor_footer.dart). Save flushes and closes; dismissing also keeps the edits.
  • Board-preferred (ED-076): one surface that grows: phones go from the medium-detent sheet (E-Phones F3, left) to the full editor by pulling up; Mac and web use the docked inspector (E-FullA-v2, no Save button); E-Minimal opens the task in the Day column.
  • Kept either way: /task/:id deep links, Move to inbox, Delete task (Trash 30 days + Undo), series "this one or all" questions, every field.

The one choice left

A (recommended): Done, not Save. Edits save as you go (as built); the top-right button says Done and only closes; a quiet "Saved · Undo" line confirms. Inspector and Day column behave the same. Cancel goes away, since it would suggest edits are thrown out when they are not.
B: keep Cancel and Save. Same autosave underneath, but the buttons stay. Cancel would have to undo everything since the sheet opened, which is new behaviour.
C: keep today's chain. Sheet, then a separate editor screen with Save.

Also decided here (ED-078)

Switching Full ↔ Minimal while editing or selecting: proposed: the open task stays open (it moves between the sheet or inspector and the Day column); its edits are already saved; a multi-selection or a drag in progress is cancelled, and Undo still works.
