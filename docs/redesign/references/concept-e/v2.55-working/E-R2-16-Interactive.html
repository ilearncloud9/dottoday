    Why this beats the built flow

    Value before set-upFour required taps from launch to your own Today (Get started, Continue, That’s my day or Skip for now, Put it on today). The built flow has seven steps.
    Ask in contextNotifications are asked right after the first task, with that task’s own reminder previewed; the system prompt follows a yes. Android 13+ and Apple guidance both ask for this.
    Your data, not a sampleThe sun and moon shape the thread, the routines you tap land on it, and the first task joins them. Nothing to unlearn, and on tablets your own Today is visible behind the card the whole time.
    One identity, native presentationSame steps and brand everywhere; Back, system dialogs, status bars, tab bars and haptics follow each platform (RD-0001).
    Accessible by constructionThe sun and moon are sliders (arrow keys, swipe up or down with VoiceOver and TalkBack); every step scrolls at 200% text; colour is never the only signal.
    No-shame and private“Not now” is never held against you. The first task is read on the device; nothing leaves it without an account.
