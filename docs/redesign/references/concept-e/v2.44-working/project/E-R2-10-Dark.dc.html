  • Two lists, by design: the system one (iPad menu bar on ⌘-hold; Android Meta+/) for people who expect the platform, and the app’s own built sheet (⌘/ or Ctrl+/) as the one complete list everywhere, including Mac, web and phones with a keyboard.
  • One scheme (ED-095, ED-248): ⌘ on iPad and Mac, Ctrl on Android and web on other hosts. All keys and labels are the built ones (app_shortcut_groups.dart); nothing new.
  • Touch frames show no key hints (R2-1); hints appear only in these lists and in menus while a keyboard is attached.
  • Open (implementation, ED-095): the iPad key commands and the Android shortcut registration need native code; both are unverified on devices. Drawing them does not make them built.
