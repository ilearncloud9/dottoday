What these approvals settle, and what they do not

  • Settled: how the phone bottom rows look and where everything sits, Full and Minimal, iPhone and Android.

3 · Native implementation questions not yet verified

None of these is approved by the bottom-row approvals

iPhone (N3) Not verified

  • Taps: +, Day ▾ and each tab on the device, including while scrolling (N2 dropped a tap; N3 has no platform view inside Flutter, so N1's result is the closer guide).
  • Hold on +: the native button's menu (Brain dump, Routine, Paste a list) handing the choice to Flutter; also exposed as accessibility actions.
  • Badge: N1/N2 kept the Inbox badge in sync over a channel; N3 uses the same channel, not yet re-checked.
  • Routes: the selected tab after relaunch and deep links (dottoday://, ED-097) when the tab bar lives in UIKit and routes in go_router.
  • Safe areas: N1 had to send insets to Flutter over a channel; N3 inherits this. Keyboard open: the row should hide or stay under the keyboard.
  • Accessibility: VoiceOver order (Day ▾, tabs, +), Full Keyboard Access focus, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast on the + and Day ▾, largest text and Large Content Viewer.
  • Capsule alignment: re-align on rotation, larger text and future iOS (rule from N3).
  • Older iOS: nothing run on iOS 16–25; no Instruments trace of iOS's own glass drawing.

Android (C) Not verified

  • Build: a Flutter widget (BackdropFilter + clipped shapes), no platform view and no new dependency. Not built or run yet.
  • Blur on 11 and older: Flutter can blur on any Android version (it draws the blur itself); the approved rule still turns it solid on 11 and older, for cost and platform look.
  • Performance: blur cost on a low-end Android 12+ phone, with the thread scrolling beneath; measure frame times.
  • Insets: gesture navigation (24 dp up) and 3-button navigation (16 dp above the system bar); edge to edge is required from Android 15.
  • Accessibility: TalkBack announces navigation items with selected state and "Inbox, 3 new"; order Day, destinations, +; 48 dp targets; keyboard and D-pad focus; font scale 200 %; high-contrast text (Android 14+) turns surfaces solid.
  • Wording: "custom Material-inspired arrangement", in code comments and docs too.

Mac sidebar Not verified

  • VoiceOver and keyboard Tab traversal in the sidebar.
  • Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast (system settings on your Mac were not changed).
  • Warm tint: not tried; the spike used the neutral system material.
