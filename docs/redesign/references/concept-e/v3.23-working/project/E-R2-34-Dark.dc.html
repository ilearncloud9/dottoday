Rules
Every answer is right
Picking a one thing is never judged; changing it is one tap.
No streaks
Past days and the year strip show what happened, with no streak, score or red.
Find, not audit
“When did I last…” answers a question; it never lists what was not done.
Share is yours
The year strip leaves the device only when you share it.
Android mirrors iPhone
The same groups and order in Material (ED-338).
Text from the app
Every string is built. Tasks and dates are examples. No new copy.
