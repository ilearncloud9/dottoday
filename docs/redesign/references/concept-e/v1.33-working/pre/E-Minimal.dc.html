Minimal hides, never removes
Where each hidden thing is reached on Mac and web. Per docs/14 §3; differences are marked.
Show everything (full mode)
Mac: View › Show Everything. Web: Search or run › Show everything, or Settings. Opens E's standard wide layout, which is an owner decision (E-Workspace, a thread layout or a full Focus layout).
Day thread
Right column by default. Hide or show: ⌥⌘I, View › Show Day Column (Mac); "Hide the day thread" (web).
Routines and Reviews
View menu or ⌘K on Mac; k finder on web. Not in the minimal sidebar.
Week, Month, Go to date, Past days
Calendar row; the date button in the toolbar; ⇧⌘T; the finder.
Counts and sync detail
The summary shows time, not counts. "Synced" stays in the toolbar (Mac) and sidebar footer (web); a banner appears when something needs you (R-08).
Brain dump
Paste several lines into Add, or Brain Dump… ⇧⌘N; the plan opens and nothing is written before Accept.
Shift the rest, +15 / +30
Need more time? on Now; right-click any dot; Day › Shift the Rest From Here….
Colour, icon, repeat, alerts, subtasks, notes
Task column › More details.
Duplicate day, Copy as text, Review, Low-energy mode
The Day menu (menu bar and the column's Day button on Mac; "Day menu" on web) and the finder.
Export, backups, Trash
Mac: File › Export…; Settings › Data. Web: Settings › Data. Both in the finder.
Inbox
Its own sidebar row with a count, ⌘2. §3 says a Later row: hypothesis · not approved
Settings
Mac: Dottoday › Settings… ⌘, in its own window. Web: sidebar row, /settings.
