C · Week of threads (new)
Not chosen · A approved (ED-252)Concept E signature at week scale
  • Today’s thread on the left; on the right the whole week as seven small threads (one per day) on a shared 7 AM–11 PM axis, today highlighted with the now-bead.
  • The Inbox sits under the week as loose dots: drag one onto any day’s thread and it lands at that time (dashed target, “Thu 11:00”). Tap a day to open it on the left.
  • Why: the thread-and-dot identity becomes the planning tool itself, no grid needed. Touch-friendly (big drop lanes), still reads day by day for screen readers (ED-248).
  • Consistency: if chosen, it should also be the Week view on Mac and web at the same width (width decides layout), replacing the card grid there.

D · Horizon desk (new)
Not chosen · A approved (ED-252)Minimal’s horizon, made Full
  • The day laid flat across the landscape screen as a large horizon (Minimal’s approved horizon, ED-063/ED-246), done tasks folded into “Earlier · 3 done”.
  • Below it, three cards: Now (the orb with Done, Focus, Need more time?), Next, and Inbox. A week strip above switches days.
  • Why: landscape tablets are held at a desk or on a stand; the whole day reads at a glance from a distance, and the current task is one tap away.
  • Trade-off: less room for dragging precise times than a grid; best as a Today view. Consistency: if chosen, the same “Today” view should exist on Mac and web at this width.
