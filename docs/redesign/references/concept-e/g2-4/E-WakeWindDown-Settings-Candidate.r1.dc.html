Recommended integration

  1. Two ends, one night. Keep Dottoday's words: Wake Up starts the day, Wind Down ends it, and the night is the span between. The dial draws the night with the moon and sun caps from the thread, in their own colours.
  2. No separate bedtime. Apple's Wind Down is a run-up before bedtime; ours is the moment the day ends. Adding a bedtime would create two evening times for one idea. If you want a lights-out time later, it would be a new feature (Wind Down + a buffer).
  3. Schedules are a view of Different days. Weekdays and Weekend are groups of weekdays with the same times; Every day covers any day without a schedule. No migration.
  4. One place. Settings › Your day › Wake Up and Wind Down holds schedules, the night (sleep target, night arc), the alarm and its reminders. Today's seven separate Day shape and Wake Up rows move here; the alarm keeps every built option.
  5. Alarm stays a phone feature. Each schedule only says whether it rings; sound and snooze are shared. Mac and web edit the same schedules and say the alarm rings on the phone.
  6. No-shame copy. "Matches / under / over your 8 h sleep target" in plain text, never orange or red, never "short".
