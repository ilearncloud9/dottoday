Rules
Moving is direct
Drag writes one move, with Undo. Shift later tasks is the one-gesture “shift everything after this” (R-04, R-05).
Hold to lift, hold to select
The same 250 ms hold lifts a row; letting go in place selects it instead, so multi-select still starts from a hold (R-05).
No shame
The Trash bin fills with ink, never red; the coral tint is the built swipe colour, not a warning.
Touch and pointer
Touch, pen and trackpad hold first; a mouse lifts at once. Hover is a flat tint, no lift (ED-116).
Same everywhere (R-13)
Phones, tablets, Mac and web share the drag; Mac and web use the pointer frames.
