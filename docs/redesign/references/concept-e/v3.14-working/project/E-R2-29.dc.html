How does Wake Up work?
Wake Up rings at the start of your day, like an alarm where your phone allows it. When you stop it, a card shows your day and starts it with one tap. If nothing has started a few minutes later, one gentle check asks. Each has its own switch in Settings › Day shape › Wake Up options.
Wake Up rings as a notification for now, so Do Not Disturb and silent mode can quiet it. Allow Alarms & reminders to ring it like an alarm.

Rules
Same features (R-13)
Everything drawn on iPhone is on Android in Material controls; only Siri & Shortcuts and Home Screen quick actions differ.
Material, not a copy
Rows, switches, dialogs, menus and snackbars are Material 3; time pickers stay the same wheel everywhere.
Privacy
Learning stays on the device; with App lock on, nothing shows in Recents or screenshots.
Calm
A failed unlock and a denied permission show no error, just the way back.
Undo
Duplicate day copies at once and offers Undo; it writes nothing else.
Text from the app
Every string is built (app_en.arb and the Android shortcut labels). Tasks and dates are examples. No new copy.
