The thread

Every surface shows the day as Dottoday shows it inside the app: a thread of dots. The task under way is a ring in its colour that fills as time passes, tasks ahead are hollow dots on a line, and the day dot in each header fills from Wake Up to Wind Down. Familiar, list-like and easy to scan. One sample moment: 10:42 AM, “Deep work: roadmap” is under way with 18 minutes left and 3 of 10 done. Light on R2-17-A.

Rules for this option
The thread and the dot
Now is a ring that fills with time used; ahead is a hollow dot on a line; done is filled. The day dot fills from Wake Up to Wind Down.
Calm by default
No overdue badges, no red, no streaks. The notification is silent and alerts once; the Live Activity ends with the task.
Native chrome
WidgetKit and Glance shapes, the Dynamic Island, Quick Settings tiles and the macOS menu keep their system form.
One snapshot, five intents
Every surface reads DaySnapshot and runs only Add task, Mark done, +15 min, Start focus and Open. None talks to Supabase (CLAUDE.md §11).
Text from the app
Only built strings: Now, Next up, Today, until {time}, {time} left, Done, +15 min, {n} in Inbox, Add task, Start focus. No new copy.
No new targets
Everything drawn exists today. A watch app, Mac widgets or a Wake Up Live Activity redesign would be new targets and need the owner first.
