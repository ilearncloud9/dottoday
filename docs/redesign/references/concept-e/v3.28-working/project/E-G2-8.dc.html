Tablet implications (recorded, not drawn)

  • iPad: Add is a popover from + at medium and wide widths (built); the segmented control sits at its top. Hold on + and ⇧⌘N with a hardware keyboard. Brain dump's proposal opens beside the day, not over it, in landscape. Pointer hover on the three segments. Slide Over and narrow Split View fall back to the phone sheet.
  • Android tablets and foldables: the FAB sits in the navigation rail at medium/expanded; its FAB menu opens beside the rail. Brain dump and Routine open as a side sheet in expanded (two panes), a bottom sheet in compact. Folded ↔ unfolded keeps the draft (it is already kept in local history). Keyboard: Ctrl+Shift+N.
  • Unverified: the proposal's placement in a two-pane layout; M3 FAB menu in a rail; dictation mic on tablets.
  • Round 2: draw these in the tablet checklist pass; phone frames here do not count as tablet designs.
