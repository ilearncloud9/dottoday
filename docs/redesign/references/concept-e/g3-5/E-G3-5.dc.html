Why A

  • It fits the lean finish: ED-022 kept shadows for floating surfaces, and a block you are about to pick up, or are dragging, is one.
  • Drag needs it most: blocks may overlap; a lifted block stays readable over the one beneath it.
  • Already drawn: A changes no board; it only records the rule (hover and drag only, never at rest).
  • Against A: B is calmer and has no shadows on the grid at all; pointer users still get the grab cursor and tint.

Platforms and tablets

  • Web: as chosen. Mac: the same Workspace grid with a pointer, so the same rule.
  • iPad and Android tablets: with a pointer or trackpad, the same hover rule; by touch, no hover (Round 2).
  • Phones: none; drag lift is in the motion spec.
