Rules
Where they live
Energy is a pill beside Repeat. Sound, Travel time and Pinned sit in the More card with Delete task (ED-086), on every platform.
One tap where it can be
Pinned is a switch, not a screen. Energy is a three-item menu. Travel time is a short ticked list.
What the day shows
A pinned task carries a lock; travel time shows as “· 15 min travel” and is not counted as free. Pinned tasks stay put when others shift or run late.
Text from the app
Every string is built. No new copy.
